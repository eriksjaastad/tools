# Retired model-bench pilot results

These six sealed pilot reports were produced by `model-bench` (card #6493) and
are retained as historical evidence only. The `model-bench` package was retired
on 2026-09-25 under card #6453: the Mac Mini benchmark effort was discontinued,
and replacement benchmark research is tracked separately (#7001).

The bench code, runners, CLI, tests, dependencies, and CI references were
removed from the active tree. Full history remains in Git. These results are
advisory evidence from their sealed run; they are not current model
recommendations.

Files:

- `pilot_hypocrisynow_sealed_detection.{json,md}`
- `pilot_hypocrisynow_sealed_expert_review.{json,md}`
- `pilot_hypocrisynow_sealed_extraction.{json,md}`

The project-owned `seats.yaml` files and the `seats.v1` contract did not live in
`_tools`; they belong to the portfolio project repos and to
`project-scaffolding` (`scaffold/seats.py`, `templates/seats.schema.v1.md`),
which is now archived.
